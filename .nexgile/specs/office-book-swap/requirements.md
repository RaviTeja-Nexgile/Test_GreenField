# Requirements: Office Book Swap

## Requirement 1: Lend and borrow books

**User Story:** As a colleague, I want to list the books I can lend and borrow others', so that we share books at work.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Colleague adds a book THE SYSTEM SHALL show it on the shelf
2. WHEN a Colleague borrows a book THE SYSTEM SHALL remind them to return it after two weeks
